# The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

> Introducing Hover, the app that watches your houseplants through a webcam and texts you the exact moment one of them sighs, because our founder is certain your fern has been holding something back since March.

Hover turns your houseplants into emotionally troubled employees. Using webcam-based AI, it detects the precise moment a plant sighs and pushes you a text so you can finally address the silent resentment your fern has been nursing since March. A problem nobody has, solved with unwavering certainty.

Built with camera AI and zero justification.
